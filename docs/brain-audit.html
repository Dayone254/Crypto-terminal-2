Findings, in priority order

CRITICAL
91.5% of symbols are scored blind. Only 34 of 402 pass the candle pre-filter, so 368 symbols feed the scorer rsi_1h=None → 50, no Bollinger, no MACD, no VWAP, no swings. Their score is effectively day-change + relative-strength + volume only.
Fix: fetch candles for every symbol that passes the volume gate (the pre-filter exists to save requests, but it is rejecting the very inputs the scorer needs).
CRITICAL
ENTRY_ZONE is assigned with the score gate bypassed. The post-ladder rule relabels anything priced within 0.75% of tranche A as ENTRY_ZONE — regardless of score. That is exactly the cohort that lost: 8 losses, 0 wins, average score 31.9 (minimum configured: 60).
Fix: require score ≥ entry_zone_score_min in the override, not just price proximity.
HIGH
Score and label contradict each other. 63.7% score ≥ 70 while 83.6% are labelled SKIP. On the dashboard, “score” therefore reads as high conviction on symbols the brain refuses to plan.
Fix: surface the binding constraint (volume / chase / no-ladder) next to the score.
HIGH
The learning loop is switched off. component_feedback has 0 rows — engine/feedback.py is never called, so no outcome ever adjusts a component weight. The brain does not learn from its 9 losses.
Fix: run feedback after each evaluator pass and feed hit-rates back into the weights.
HIGH
Direction is effectively one-way. 86.6% SHORT, and all actionable labels SHORT. With RSI defaulted to 50 for 92% of symbols, direction is decided almost entirely by day_change and rs_vs_btc — in a broadly red market that mechanically selects SHORT everywhere.
Fix: restore candle inputs (above) and re-check the LONG/SHORT picker once RSI/MACD are real.
MEDIUM
Reward is degenerate at 2.0R. Every ladder lands exactly on the minimum R/R floor, so the L2-wall front-running and gamma-wall capping are not adding reward.
Fix: verify target-1 interception is actually firing (needs L2 data, which needs candles).
MEDIUM
Nonsensical price data in the ledger. PAXG-USD (gold, ~$4,000) has an entry of exactly $10.00, TP $12.00, SL $8.00 — a placeholder, not a level.
Fix: validate ladder prices against spot before persisting a signal.
MEDIUM
48% of all scan runs failed historically (72 FAILED / 149 total). The commit and lock fixes address the cause — worth watching that this ratio drops.
Fix: already applied; monitor.
